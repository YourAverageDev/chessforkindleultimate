Pair This Device

To play as yourself on this Kindle, you need to link it to your Lichess account from another device. Here's how:

1. On your phone or computer, open this same site in a browser.
2. Log in with Lichess there.
3. On that device, find and tap "Link Another Device".
4. Enter the code below when it asks for the pairing code.
